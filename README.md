### About

I build software for problems I've actually seen up close — small
businesses tracking attendance on paper registers, truck owners doing
hisaab in notebooks, landlords chasing rent over phone calls. No
formal CS degree — I learned by shipping, breaking things in
production, and fixing them at 2 AM.

Lately I've been pulled into local-first AI — the idea that you
shouldn't need to send your data to someone else's cloud to get useful
work out of a model. JellyClaw is where that met everything else I
build for.

It's me and two collaborators — no funding round, no big team. One
working product at a time, built in the open.
