### 👋 About

I'm a self-taught developer building software for problems I've actually
seen up close — small businesses tracking attendance on paper registers,
truck owners doing hisaab in notebooks, landlords chasing rent over phone
calls. No formal CS degree — I learned by shipping, breaking things in
production, and fixing them at 2 AM.

I started out building for the Indian SMB market because that's the world
I grew up around — cash-strapped, WhatsApp-first, distrustful of anything
that feels like "enterprise software." Somewhere along the way I got
pulled into local-first AI — the idea that you shouldn't need to send
your data to someone else's cloud to get useful work out of a model.
JellyClaw is where those two interests collided.

I'm not chasing funding or a big team right now — it's me and two
collaborators, building in the open, one working product at a time.
